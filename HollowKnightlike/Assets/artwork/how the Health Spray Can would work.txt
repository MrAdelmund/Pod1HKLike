This health system works similar to Silksong's health regeneration but much more weird.

Each can repersents 3 hit points, crumpling more with each hit until its fully destroyed and falls away while also fading away. 

If the player has no more spray cans, they DIE.

The player starts out with 1 spray can, but 2 shelf slots that can hold these spray cans. 

By charging up for a while you can add another spray can on the right of the currently rightmost spray can. 

The rightmost spray can is always the one drained whenever the player is hit.

The player can find more shelf slots in the world allowing them to hold more spray cans and have more hp.

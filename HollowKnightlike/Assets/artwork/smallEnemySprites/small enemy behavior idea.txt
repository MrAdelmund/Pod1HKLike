The Small enemy (who I'll call Employed from now on) works like a mixture of a goomba and a pilgrim groveler from silksong.

They start out just idly walking around the area unless the player is nearby in which case they'll start walking towards the player. 

Once they get close enough to the player they'll reel back for a moment before throwing their body at the player in an attempt to deal some contact damage.

Once they hit the floor they'll eat the dirt and stay there for a few seconds giving the player time to hurt them before they quickly get back up and get ready to attack again.

They can fall off of ledges if they're knocked into them, in which case they'll fall down and eat the dirt again once hitting the ground.

Once their HP is fully drained they'll stagger backwards glitching out before exploding into 5 bits of "gore", the two halves of their head, their two feet, and their torso which all fall off screen.
